LeRobot Rollout Timing Charts

Visualizes per-frame timing logs from lerobot-rollout. The charts separate ACT chunk refills from cheap cached-action frames, which is the key pattern in your latest MPS run.

In the pasted log, source=model is misleading: sync inference calls ACTPolicy.select_action every frame, but most calls only pop an already-computed action from ACT's internal queue. This report classifies a frame as chunk refill when loop, engine, or policy timing shows a real chunk computation.

Interpretation

Pattern The run shows one expensive initial warmup/refill, then a repeating pattern of many cheap cached-action frames followed by a roughly 45-55 ms chunk refill.
Camera and robot I/O obs stays near 1-2 ms and send stays near 0.1-0.2 ms, so those are not the bottleneck in this log.
MPS timing caveat With LEROBOT_ROLLOUT_TIMING_SYNC_DEVICE=0, MPS stage attribution is approximate. Trust loop for end-to-end control timing; treat policy and post split as a useful hint, not exact causality.
Current diagnosis The system is generally fast enough between refills. The only frames over the 25 FPS budget are ACT chunk refills, especially the first warmup frame.
